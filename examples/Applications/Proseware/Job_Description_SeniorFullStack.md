**About Proseware**

Proseware builds AI agents that draft, review, and track contracts for in-house legal teams. Our customers are the two- and three-person legal departments at mid-size companies who review hundreds of vendor agreements a quarter. We're about 140 people, Series B, and our agents now review more than 50,000 contracts a month.

**About the role**

You'll join the Agent Workspace team, which owns the product surface where lawyers work alongside our agents: the review queue, the redline editor, and the approval flows that decide what an agent may do on its own. You'll ship across the stack, from React interfaces to the Node.js services that call our models, and you'll own the quality bar for features that people trust with real legal risk.
