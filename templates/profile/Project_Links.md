## Notes
- When a project gets a public URL, add it here and update its project block in the Final resume in `Resumes/Final/`.
- Only link projects that are live and presentable. Never add a localhost, staging, or placeholder URL. Why: a recruiter who clicks a broken link remembers it.
- Every link on the resume must be a real, clickable hyperlink in the PDF. Check it after rendering.
